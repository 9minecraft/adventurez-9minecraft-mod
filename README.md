# AdventureZ — Battle Deadly Bosses Across Every Dimension

AdventureZ 9minecraft Mod populates your worlds with dozens of new beasts, spellcasters, and giant bosses across every dimension. Hostile monsters guard rare loot in camps and caves, while peaceful wildlife roams grassy plains and icy tundras. Defeating these foes gives you materials to craft strong armor and weapons for your journey. The End and the Nether also receive dangerous new threats that test your combat gear.

Spawns dozens of unique creatures and bosses across the Overworld, Nether, and End.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Globox1997**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
